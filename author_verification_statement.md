## 1. Statement of verification

I, the author, declare that I have personally executed and verified the computational pipeline
reported in the manuscript and deposited in the repository above. Specifically:

1. **All input datasets are public and were downloaded by the deposited scripts from their original
   repositories** (NCBI GEO for transcriptomic data; ChEMBL/EMBL-EBI for drug and bioactivity data;
   RCSB PDB for protein structures; the Broad Institute Drug Repurposing Hub for the approved-drug
   list). **No primary data were generated by me**, and no human or animal subjects were involved.

2. **Every numerical value reported in the manuscript is generated by the deposited scripts** and is
   traceable to a file under `results/tables/` or to the machine-generated phase reports
   (`results/P2_RESULTS.md` … `results/P6_RESULTS.md`). I have re-run the pipeline end-to-end and
   confirmed that the deposited scripts reproduce the reported tables.

3. **The docking engine and version are recorded**: AutoDock Vina 1.2.5, invoked with a fixed
   `--seed 42` and recorded `exhaustiveness` / `max_evals` values, which are also written into every
   docking result table (`exh`, `max_evals` audit columns). The screening parameters are not silently
   changed between runs; the parameter-fidelity validation that motivated their choice is part of the
   deposited results.

4. **Methodological controls reported in the manuscript were executed, not assumed.** These include:
   - a docking-parameter fidelity validation conducted *before* the screen;
   - synthetic-data self-tests of the downstream scoring code conducted *before* the long docking runs;
   - an independently labelled positive control using ChEMBL measured binding activity (labels
     independent of the docking predictions; no double-dipping);
   - a molecular-weight confounder control applied to every enrichment claim, with a size-only
     baseline reported alongside;
   - a face-validity test that **failed** and is reported as a negative result rather than being
     omitted;
   - a pocket-volume confound test and a target-ranking stability test under library expansion.

5. **Negative and null results are reported.** Where a test did not support the expectation (for
   example, the face-validity analysis and the human-side corroboration layer), this is stated
   explicitly in the manuscript, with the corresponding numbers, instead of being dropped.

6. **No fabricated, imputed or hand-edited values appear in any result table.** Where a step
   could not be completed, the affected claim is marked as such in the manuscript rather than
   filled with an estimate.

7. **Statistical discipline.** Pseudoreplication was avoided by construction: drug-level aggregation
   for screening enrichment, and sample-level (not cell-level) tests for single-cell composition and
   expression. Cross-dataset meta-analysis used effect-size weighting rather than pooling raw
   sample counts, and leave-one-dataset-out evaluation was used for the machine-learning models.

8. **Author identity and repository hygiene.** I confirm that the research GitHub account used for
   this project is `yyx-4113`. No other account is associated with this work, and I have verified
   that no unrelated repositories are referenced in the manuscript or the repository.

## 2. Limitations acknowledged

- Drug-docking scores are not binding free energies. With the low-budget docking setting used, rank
  fidelity has a measured ceiling, so results are framed as candidate prioritisation rather than
  precise ranking.
- A single crystal conformation per target was used; induced fit, membrane environment and explicit
  water were not modelled.
- Raw docking scores are not comparable across targets; target prioritisation therefore uses
  within-target percentiles combined with druggability priors.
- Human direct validation is absent from the public data used here; all nominated candidates remain
  hypotheses pending wet-lab validation.
