===== L136 (len=1057) =====
The honest docking null is silent, not negative, on the validated non-opioid analgesic classes. The entire ion-channel analgesic class — α2δ subunits (gabapentinoid targets) and NaV channels (including the clinically validated NaV1.7/NaV1.8) — was structurally undockable (no ligand-anchored pocket for a defined docking box) and was therefore never screened, so the null deliberately cannot speak to those mechanisms; absence of docking enrichment is not evidence against gabapentinoid or NaV-channel action³⁸. Beyond this, the work is a secondary reanalysis of public data: it carries no new wet-lab measurements, the human layer is a blood-proxy (n = 60, underpowered), no dataset captures established chronic postsurgical pain (>3 months; the incision arm models acute-to-subacute pain), DRG-resident macrophages are a recognised neuroimmune sentinel after nerve injury²⁶ yet the present bulk-tissue signal cannot resolve microglia from infiltrating macrophages, and the 35 hubs are a resampling-sensitive candidate set requiring prospective validation.

===== L140 (len=1637) =====
In this multi-dataset in-silico reanalysis, the DRG-centred nerve-injury axis (with spinal-cord localisation) is a nerve-injury-associated, heterogeneity-sensitive transcriptional response; on the available evidence it is not established as CPSP-specific — the translation test to the sole surgical-incision arm was non-significant (46.2% vs 47.1% background, p = 0.14) and underpowered, and the inference is provisional and does not demonstrate either CPSP-specificity or its absence.
