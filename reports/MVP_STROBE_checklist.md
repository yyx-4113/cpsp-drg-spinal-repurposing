# STROBE Checklist — Observational Reanalysis of Public Transcriptomes

**Manuscript:** *Conserved nerve-injury-associated transcriptional response on the DRG–spinal axis: dorsal root ganglion analysis with spinal-cord localisation and honest repurposing null*

**STROBE statement version:** STROBE 2007 (von Elm et al., *Lancet* 2007;370:1453–1457). This is a **secondary, in-silico reanalysis** of publicly deposited transcriptomic data. The only primary human observational dataset reanalysed here is **GSE158825** (n = 60 human plasma miRNA; lumbar surgery with a pain-outcome measure), which is an observational cohort with a pain phenotype. The remaining 11 GEO datasets are animal-model or in-vitro transcriptomes and are not primary human-observational studies; they are re-used here as external-mechanism consistency evidence, not as the STROBE-indexed cohort.

**Transparency note:** Because the present work is a reanalysis of *de-identified* public data, items that concern the original study design, recruitment, setting, exposure ascertainment timing, and follow-up are **not under the control of this reanalysis** and are marked **N/A (primary deposition)** — the relevant design decisions reside in the original GEO depositions (GSE158825: IRB-approved, informed-consent documentation per the manuscript Ethics statement). Items concerning the reanalysis *methods, statistics, and reporting* are addressed in the manuscript and cited by section.
